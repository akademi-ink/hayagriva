<<===== MODE =====<<

Citation numbers depend on the bibliography sort among the entries known so
far. citeproc-js numbers lazily: the first citation only knows the entry it
cites, so it renders `[1]` even though an alphabetically earlier entry is
cited later. Once that entry is known, the numbering shifts (F-119).

>>===== RESULT =====>>
[1]
[1, 2]
[1]
[1, 3]
<<===== RESULT =====<<
