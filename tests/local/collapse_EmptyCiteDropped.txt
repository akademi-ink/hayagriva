>>===== MODE =====>>
citation
<<===== MODE =====<<

Focused empty-cite semantics in a collapsed citation (F-129): an item whose
author was collapsed and which has no date renders nothing, so the cite is
dropped together with its preceding delimiter while the layout affixes
survive. A per-item suffix keeps the cite alive. Expectations verified
against citeproc-js 2.4.63.

>>===== RESULT =====>>
(Smith 2000)
(Smith 2000, 2001)
(Smith 2000, ;)
<<===== RESULT =====<<
