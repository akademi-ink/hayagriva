>>===== MODE =====>>
citation
<<===== MODE =====<<

Focused `after-collapse-delimiter` splice semantics for `collapse="year"`
(F-128): a collapsed continuation keeps the layout delimiter, every other
splice takes the after-collapse delimiter, and a locator on the preceding
cite forces the after-collapse delimiter even before a continuation.
Expectations verified against citeproc-js 2.4.63.

>>===== RESULT =====>>
(Alpha 2000, 2001; Beta 2002)
(Alpha 2000, 9; 2001; Beta 2002)
(Beta 2002; Gamma 2003)
